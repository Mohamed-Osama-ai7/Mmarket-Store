# Catalogue price calibration references

Checked: 20 October 2026. Public Egypt retail listings were used to set plausible illustrative EGP ranges. Mmarket product names and descriptions are original; Mmarket is not represented as affiliated with these retailers. Retail prices vary by collection, promotion, size, stock, and season.

- LC Waikiki Egypt, men's T-shirts and polo styles: https://www.lcwaikiki.eg/en/men-t-shirts-t-345 — examples visible during review included EGP 299, 349, 399, 449, 499, 599, 699, 799, 899, and selected styles at EGP 999.
- LC Waikiki Egypt, men's jeans: https://www.lcwaikiki.eg/en/men-jeans-t-194 — examples visible during review included common denim around EGP 999–1,499 and some premium styles above that range.
- LC Waikiki Egypt, men's shirts: https://www.lcwaikiki.eg/en/men-shirts-t-158 — examples visible during review included shirts around EGP 699–1,499, with some shirt-jackets near EGP 1,999.

Prices in `Backend/scripts/seed-catalog.js` are illustrative demo/launch values broadly calibrated to these market examples. Prices for other categories are estimates, not verified supplier quotes. Review sourcing, margins, image licensing, consumer disclosures, tax obligations, and return policies before accepting real public orders.
